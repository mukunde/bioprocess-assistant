## Application captures and handbook excerpts

Four genuine Bioprocess Assistant states were captured through browser automation on 2026-11-01. Images were saved directly as JPEG; their pixels were not edited. Screenshot sizing is reported in the manifest. Target rectangles were measured from the application DOM in viewport percentages; the result hotspot is enlarged vertically for usability. Spotlight and pink overlays are rendered separately by the player.

The captured application includes the local, uncommitted MabSelect journey. The repository HEAD alone does not identify that application state; the manifest therefore records the relevant source-file hashes separately. This tour is standalone and does not include those application changes.
