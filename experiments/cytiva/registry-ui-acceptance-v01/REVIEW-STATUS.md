## Retained reservations

- Keep alternatives, product scopes, quantities, minimum durations, immediate
  follow-up washes and buffer pH associations distinct; never invent parameters.
- Keep detergent and solvent routes separate. Increasing gradients address air
  bubbles; lower flow addresses backpressure. Neither supplies a numeric pressure.
- Pressure absence is limited to selected admissible excerpts, not the whole
  manual or manufacturer specifications. Never substitute an unrequested procedure.
- Ask only for missing/conflicting information; keep supplied fields and permit
  uncertainty without demanding a confirmed scientific diagnosis. Clarification
  is an application policy supported by documentary distinctions.
- Clarifying a product does not guarantee a pressure value will become available.
- Each expected emitted citation must resolve to the exact admissible source and
  retain its scoped reservations. Human-review-only excerpts must not become
  fabricated delivered citations. UA03 and UA08 expect no emitted citation.
- Outside-release abstention does not establish incompatibility, unsafe sample
  use, a purification requirement or permission to use a substitute product.
