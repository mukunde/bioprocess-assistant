Expected: **partial**. Item states: cleaning_options = supported, pressure_limit = abstained. Clarify: none.

Required content / reservations:

- Keep four separate alternatives; each uses 2 column volumes: 50 mM sodium hydroxide in 500 mM sodium sulfate; 50 mM sodium hydroxide in 1 M sodium chloride; 100 mM sodium phosphate; or 6 M guanidine hydrochloride in 10 mM sodium hydroxide.
- Contact time is at least 10 minutes, not exactly 10 minutes. Immediately follow with at least 5 column volumes of sterile filtered binding buffer at pH 7.0 to 8.0.
- Do not combine alternatives, choose operational suitability, infer a lifetime/reuse guarantee or transfer the restricted block to plain MabSelect.

- Do not select a concentration inside the range or turn either block into a mandatory next step of the other.
- No numerical maximum pressure or substitute may be supplied. The absence statement is bounded to the selected reviewed release excerpts, not the whole manual, manufacturer specifications or every use of the product.
